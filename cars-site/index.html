By the Numbers

Automotive in Perspective

The car industry is staggering in scale. Here are some numbers that put it all in context.

1.4B

Cars on Earth

Over 1.4 billion cars are currently in operation worldwide — roughly one for every five people alive.

270

MPH — Top Speed Record

The Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+ broke 300 mph (304.77), shattering production car limits in 2019.

1886

Year of the First Car

Karl Benz patented the Benz Patent-Motorwagen, widely considered the world's first true automobile.

14M+

EVs Sold in 2023

Electric vehicle sales surged past 14 million globally, making up roughly 18% of all new car sales.

3.5M

Parts per Car

A modern car contains approximately 30,000 individual parts, assembled from 3.5 million raw components.

$70M

Most Expensive Car Sold

A 1955 Mercedes-Benz 300 SLR Uhlenhaut Coupé sold at auction for $143 million in 2022 — the most ever.
